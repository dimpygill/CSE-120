Digital Logic Gates: More Practical Questions and Complete Solutions

Eight additional scenarios continuing the earlier tutorial format. Covers XOR, XNOR, NAND, NOR, and combinations of gates. Each scenario includes a question, input definitions, complete truth table, Boolean expression, visual circuit diagram, and step-by-step reasoning.

Notation: · = AND, + = OR, ¬ = NOT, ⊕ = XOR. 0 = OFF/FALSE and 1 = ON/TRUE. XOR is 1 when inputs differ; XNOR is 1 when inputs match. NAND is NOT-AND; NOR is NOT-OR.
